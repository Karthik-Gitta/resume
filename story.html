The Story So Far

“My path here was never a straight highway. It was a series of conscious detours, unexpected discoveries, and an unwavering belief that every experience adds a valuable chapter to the story.”

Chapter 01 • 2024–2025

The Pivot: Beyond JEE Equations

Like hundreds of thousands of ambitious students in India, my high school years began within the high-gravity orbit of competitive science coaching. For months, my days were governed by thermodynamics, rotational inertia, and coordinate geometry. You learn to solve problems in neat, closed systems where every variable is defined and every equation has a predetermined answer.

Yet as I worked through stacks of mock tests, I realized an uncomfortable truth: while I appreciated the analytical rigor, the rigid predictability felt suffocating. I didn’t just want to calculate the trajectory of an object in a vacuum. I was fascinated by the messy, vibrant world of human behavior, economic incentives, and business creation.

The Realization: Why do some ideas spread like wildfire while better-engineered ones fade away? The answer was rooted in psychology, communication, distribution, and storytelling.

Choosing to step off the conventional engineering track wasn’t easy. It invited puzzled looks from peers and skeptical questions. But staying true to my own curiosity required trading comfortable defaults for a bolder trajectory: entrepreneurship, digital business, and human leadership.

Chapter 02 • 2022–2024

1,000 Voices, Stage Lights & The Backstage

Long before studying business models at IIM Bangalore, my foundational training in leadership took place in the corridors and auditoriums of RBVRR High School in Nizamabad.

In Class 9, I was entrusted with the role of Cultural Minister, and in Class 10, I was elected Head Boy, representing a student body of more than 1,000 students. Leadership in a school environment isn't theoretical; it is raw, immediate, and intensely human.

As Head Boy, I was the bridge between eager students, an exacting school administration, and external guests. I coordinated daily morning assemblies, managed inter-house rivalries, and learned how to resolve conflicts before they escalated.

The Thrill of the Microphone: Serving as Master of Ceremonies and chief student host at major institutional festivals taught me that public speaking is about creating an atmosphere where everyone feels engaged, heard, and part of something bigger.

More importantly, I fell in love with what happens behind the curtains: managing audio-visual equipment when cables fail five minutes before the chief guest arrives, keeping nervous performers calm, negotiating logistical timelines with vendors, and keeping 1,000 students attentive.

Chapter 03 • 2026

The 99.74 Leap & IIM Bangalore

When you make an unconventional pivot, you carry the responsibility of validating your own conviction. I channeled the discipline I had built during my science days into preparing for the CUET General Aptitude Test (GAT).

When the results were published, the score card read: 99.74 percentile.

That distinction unlocked the door to my dream: joining the Bachelor of Business Administration in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship (DBE) at the prestigious Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB).

Finding My Tribe: At 17, walking into an institution like IIM Bangalore is both humbling and electrifying. Here, people encourage you to cross boundaries between tech, venture capital, design, and economics.

The BBA DBE program is tailor-made for the modern digital era. It isn’t about memorizing 30-year-old management textbooks; it’s about understanding scalable software economics, founder mindsets, digital ecosystems, and real-world execution.

Chapter 04 • Present & Beyond

The Generalist's Creed: Why Breadth Is A Superpower

People often recite the first half of the famous quote: “A jack of all trades is a master of none...” as if it were a cautionary tale. But they conveniently forget the punchline: “...but oftentimes better than a master of one.”

In a world increasingly dominated by narrow specialists and automated tools, the real magic happens at the intersections. When you understand the logic of code, the nuance of financial models, the psychology of human motivation, and the craft of storytelling, you can connect dots that hyper-specialists rarely see.

Today, as a first-year undergraduate, I’m deliberately nurturing my breadth across startup ecosystems, storytelling and mythology, web development, and human relations.

My guiding philosophy remains simple: unshakable optimism. Whatever challenges or unexpected turns come my way, I know that everything happens for the best, and every single step prepares you for the chapters yet unwritten.
